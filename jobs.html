Available Positions

Community Support Worker

Reference: CS4A7B

Salary: $60,000 – $70,000 per year

Reports to: Community Services Manager

Position Description

Community Connect is seeking a compassionate and reliable Community Support Worker to assist individuals and families accessing community services. The role involves providing practical support, connecting people with appropriate services and helping clients participate in community activities.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Provide practical and emotional support to community members.
  2. Assist clients in accessing appropriate support services.
  3. Maintain accurate and confidential client records.
  4. Support community activities, workshops and events.
  5. Work with other staff and community organisations to meet client needs.

Essential Requirements

  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work respectfully with people from diverse backgrounds.
  • Good organisation and time-management skills.
  • Ability to maintain confidentiality and professional boundaries.
  • Current Working with Children Check or willingness to obtain one.

Preferable Requirements

  • Certificate or diploma in community services or a related field.
  • Previous experience in a community services environment.
  • First Aid certification.
  • Experience working with vulnerable individuals or families.

Volunteer Coordinator

Reference: VO82C5

Salary: $65,000 – $75,000 per year

Reports to: Community Programs Manager

Position Description

We are looking for an organised and enthusiastic Volunteer Coordinator to recruit, support and manage volunteers across our community programs. The successful candidate will help volunteers find suitable opportunities and ensure they have a positive and rewarding experience.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Recruit and register new volunteers.
  2. Match volunteers with suitable community programs and events.
  3. Provide volunteer induction and ongoing support.
  4. Maintain accurate volunteer records and schedules.
  5. Organise volunteer appreciation and engagement activities.

Essential Requirements

  • Excellent communication and people skills.
  • Strong organisational and administrative abilities.
  • Ability to work with people from a wide range of backgrounds.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and deadlines.
  • Confidence using common office and communication software.

Preferable Requirements

  • Previous experience coordinating volunteers.
  • Experience working in a not-for-profit organisation.
  • Experience organising community events.
  • Knowledge of volunteer management practices.

Community Programs Officer

Reference: CP6D9E

Salary: $68,000 – $80,000 per year

Reports to: Programs and Services Manager

Position Description

Community Connect is seeking a Community Programs Officer to help plan, coordinate and promote programs that respond to local community needs. This role will work with staff, volunteers and community partners to deliver accessible activities and events.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Assist with planning and delivering community programs.
  2. Coordinate community events, workshops and information sessions.
  3. Work with volunteers and community partners.
  4. Promote programs and events to community members.
  5. Collect feedback and assist with evaluating program outcomes.

Essential Requirements

  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Excellent planning and organisational skills.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with community members.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Commitment to inclusion, accessibility and community wellbeing.

Preferable Requirements

  • Qualification in community development, social services or a related field.
  • Previous experience coordinating community programs.
  • Experience organising public events or workshops.
  • Knowledge of local community services and support networks.
