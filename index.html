Welcome to Community Connect

Connecting communities through a variety of volunteering and support services

Our company slogan: A community, A click away, depicts, Community Connect as a not-for-profit organisation supporting local community programs. We coordinate volunteers, run public events, manage donations and connect people with support services. Our small digital team is growing, and we're looking for people who care about those around us.

If you're interested in volunteering or learning more about our programs, please visit our Apply page or contact us directly at info@communityconnect.org.au.

Our Services

We deliver a small number of programs, well. Each one is designed around a specific community need, from everyday volunteering through to emergency support and public events.
