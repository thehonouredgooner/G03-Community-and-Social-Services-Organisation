Welcome to Community Connect

Connecting communities through a variety of volunteering and support services

Our company slogan:A community, A click away

Community Connect is a not-for-profit organisation supporting local community programs. We coordinate volunteers, run public events, manage donations and connect people with support services. Our small digital team is growing, and we're looking for people who care about doing that work well.

If you're interested in volunteering or learning more about our programs, please visit our Apply page or contact us directly.

Our Services

  • Volunteer matching and support
  • Community program coordination
  • Event planning and management
  • Local support services and resources
